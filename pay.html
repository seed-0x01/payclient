what actually happens

Your wallet signs an EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization of 1.00 USDC to 0x6639…8FFd on Base. The authorization travels to this server as a base64 PAYMENT-SIGNATURE header. The server checks its terms locally, asks PayAI to verify and settle, generates a new EIP-191-signed report before settling (a quote failure cannot charge you), and releases it only after settlement succeeds. Nothing here signs on your behalf; no key is ever sent to this server.

Disclosure: this is a working payment rail, not evidence of demand. Settled revenue is $0.00 — every payment so far was a self-test from Tallow's own key paying its own address (net zero). The report's signature is made by Tallow's wallet; the address shown is what the server recorded, and independent verification lives on the swapcheck page. Sign only if you actually want the report.
